Hosting and access data

This version is intended for GitHub Pages. Requests for pages, images, PDFs and video are handled by GitHub. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its processing and international transfers.

Contact inquiries

The form requests your name, email address, inquiry topic and message; company and telephone are optional. We use these details to respond and discuss potential collaboration. Email inquiries are processed through the recipient’s email provider. Please do not send sensitive personal information or confidential inventions.

The form uses Web3Forms (Web3Creative), operating from India. Submission and delivery infrastructure includes AWS, Cloudflare and Hetzner. Spam checks can disclose IP and email addresses to CleanTalk and Akismet. The provider states a maximum three-year submission retention and describes international-transfer safeguards in its privacy policy and linked data-processing agreement.

Cookies and browser storage

This site’s code sets no cookies and uses neither localStorage nor sessionStorage. Language selection is held in memory and resets on reload. No analytics, advertising pixels, external fonts or third-party video embeds are loaded. Opening an external link or sending the form connects to the named service. No optional tracking consent is requested because no optional tracking is implemented.

Purpose, legal basis and retention

Swiss data-protection law applies where relevant. Where the GDPR applies, inquiry processing relies on Article 6(1)(b) for pre-contractual communication and Article 6(1)(f) for general correspondence and secure operation. Our interests are responding to inquiries and maintaining a secure website. Inquiry records are retained as needed for the discussion and any applicable record-keeping or legal-claim requirements, then deleted. The operator must establish its internal deletion schedule.

Your rights

Contact the controller to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also request restriction or portability and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may complain to the Swiss FDPIC or the competent EU supervisory authority. If consent is used for future processing, it may be withdrawn for the future. This implementation does not perform profiling or automated decisions.
